Our Journey Through Scripture

After Paul's epistles we move to the four Gospels, Matthew, Mark, Luke and John, to encounter the life and teaching of Jesus. Beyond that we go back to the foundations in the Old Testament, following God's redemptive story from Genesis to Malachi. Alongside the books, topical studies trace single themes across the whole of Scripture. Everything here stays free.

Free to use. These guides are free for personal, family and church study. Copy them, print them, teach from them.

Your notes stay with you. Notes and progress are saved in your own browser on your own device. Nothing is uploaded and no account is needed.
